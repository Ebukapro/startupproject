## Combined Recommendation — Top 8

Ranked by strength on both dimensions together (clean linguistic PASS + EASY findability first, then best of the CAUTION/WORKABLE tier):

1. **Talvinia** — PASS/EASY, the strongest all-round: intuitive place-name rhythm, distinct, supports "Talvinians" as a user-noun.
2. **Beaconlot** — PASS/EASY.
3. **Lanternlot** — PASS/EASY, plus genuine warm cultural resonance (lanterns = home light during outages).
4. **Levelbasis** — PASS/EASY, cleanest "fairness" positioning.
5. **Fairtenor** — PASS/EASY, pleasant church-choir association fits a warm brand.
6. **Lanterntally** — PASS/EASY (minor consonant-cluster flow caution only).
7. **Ridgetally** — PASS linguistically, WORKABLE on findability only because of Ridge Wallet's active trademark — worth a quick trademark-clearance check before committing, since the compound itself is otherwise clean.
8. **Lanternbasis** — CAUTION (a bit formal/cold) but EASY findability; edges out Ferrylot (CAUTION on the ferry/fairy mishearing, which hits the spelling test harder) for the last slot.

Note: 3 of the top 8 share the "Lantern-" root — good sign for the root's warmth, but worth deciding whether the company wants one flagship "Lantern" name or to diversify. Hard excludes: **Evenbasis** and **Quietparity** (FAIL, real misspelling traps — "Evan Basis"/"Quiet Parody"), and **Rondevia** (FAIL/HARD — sounds like the existing dating app Rondevo).
