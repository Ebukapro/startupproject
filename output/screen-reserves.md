Spanish/Portuguese "paritaria" (labor-negotiation term) is a faint, harmless echo. Pronunciation is stable across NG and US/UK speakers ("pa-ri-TAH-na"), fully phonetic, low mis-spelling risk on a voice note.
5. **Findability:** EASY — unique string, no competing brand, no adjacent-industry noise.
6. **App Store draft:** Title: **Paritana** (8/30) · Subtitle: **"Invest in NGX stocks with ease"** (30/30)

**Verdict: STRONG** — clean, phonetic, warm, and carries a quiet positive Yoruba echo.
**Biggest weakness:** generic "-ana" invented-fintech-suffix pattern; slightly low distinctiveness/memorability versus a coined word with a sharper hook.

## 2. Tranchera

1. **Trademark/company:** No exact hit for "Tranchera." But its root "Tranche" is actively used by **Tranche Finance**, a live DeFi CDO protocol (SLICE token, built by Jibrel, launched 2021) — real category-adjacent name overlap in the finance/crypto space, even without a direct string conflict. "Tranche" is also a live structured-finance term generally (CDO tranches), which carries a faint 2008-financial-crisis echo for finance-literate readers.
2. **App Store:** 0 exact hits via iTunes API. Clear.
3. **Association risk:** Moderate — CDO/"tranche" terminology is tied in public memory to the 2008 crash; low salience for the target 22-40 first-time-investor audience, but a real tail-risk if a journalist ever runs the etymology.
4. **Linguistic:** No meaning in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, Swahili, Arabic. Loosely echoes Spanish/Portuguese "trinchera" (trench) — neutral. **Pronunciation splits by audience**: Nigerian English speakers are likely to say "Tran-CHE-ra"; US/UK speakers more likely say "Tran-SHARE-a" or "Tran-CHAIR-a" (English "-che-" is genuinely ambiguous between "ch" and "sh"). This is a real problem given the brief weighs both accents equally.
5. **Findability:** WORKABLE — exact string is unique, but shares conceptual space with an active DeFi protocol, which will dilute some search/organic association.
6. **App Store draft:** Title: **Tranchera** (10/30) · Subtitle: **"Simple NGX stock investing app"** (30/30)

**Verdict: USABLE** — the finance-root pun is clever but double-edged.
**Biggest weakness:** genuine cross-accent pronunciation instability ("ch" vs "sh"), compounded by root-level overlap with a live DeFi "Tranche" project and CDO/crisis baggage.

## 3. Wharflot

1. **Trademark/company:** No hits found. Clear.
2. **App Store:** 0 exact hits via iTunes API. Clear.
3. **Association risk:** None found.
4. **Linguistic:** No specific meaning in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, Swahili, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Spanish. The visible substring "**war**" inside "Wh-AR-flot" is an unfortunate optical/aural read for a warm, approachable finance brand. The "wh" is silent for most English speakers (NG and US/UK alike collapse it to "War-flot"), and "flot" is not a real word (best case it's a stretch-reference to stock-market "flotation" — too subtle for anyone to pick up unprompted).
5. **Spelling test:** FAILS — hearing "Warflot"/"Wharflot" once in a voice note, most listeners will not intuit the silent "h" and will type "Warflot," "Wharflott," or "Worflot." For a bootstrapped, word-of-mouth-only growth strategy this is disqualifying.
6. **Findability:** Nominally EASY on uniqueness, but real recall/search traffic will fracture across misspellings, defeating the purpose.
7. **App Store draft:** Title: **Wharflot** (8/30) · Subtitle: **"Trade NGX stocks, made simple"** (30/30)

**Verdict: REJECT**
**Biggest weakness:** unspellable-from-hearing name (silent "wh" + invented "flot") is fatal for a strategy that depends entirely on word of mouth.

## 4. Kilnledger

1. **Trademark/company:** No exact hit for "Kilnledger." But it fully embeds **"Ledger"** — one of the best-known brand names in crypto/fintech (Ledger SAS, the hardware-wallet company), a mark that is actively and aggressively defended. It also embeds **"Kiln"**, a real, funded crypto-staking infrastructure company (kiln.fi, Paris, founded 2018) operating in adjacent fintech/crypto territory, plus unrelated registered "KILN" marks (Kiln Enterprises Ltd, Kiln Holdings Inc.). This is the highest legal-exposure candidate of the six.
2. **App Store:** 0 exact hits for "Kilnledger" via iTunes API, but "Ledger"-branded apps dominate any adjacent search.
3. **Association risk:** Moderate-high — not fraud-adjacent, but real confusion/enforcement risk from the Ledger brand.
4. **Linguistic:** "Kiln" (pottery/brick oven) is a fairly obscure English word — commonly mispronounced with a silent "n" (/kɪl/), which invites the compound to be misheard/misread as **"Kill Ledger"** — a jarring, violent reading for a "warm, approachable" financial brand. No specific meaning in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, Swahili, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Spanish.
5. **Findability:** HARD — the "Ledger" substring means any organic SEO effort is conceptually competing with a globally dominant crypto brand; the exact string is unique but the search *neighborhood* is owned by someone else.
6. **App Store draft:** Title: **Kilnledger** (11/30) · Subtitle: **"Track and grow NGX investments"** (30/30)

**Verdict: REJECT**
**Biggest weakness:** contains "Ledger" whole — real trademark/confusion exposure against a major, litigious crypto brand — compounded by a "Kill Ledger" misreading risk.

6. **Findability:** WORKABLE — the compound is uncontested, though the generic word "Tally" alone is contested territory.
7. **App Store draft:** Title: **Quiettally** (11/30) · Subtitle: **"Calm, simple NGX stock trading"** (30/30)

**Biggest weakness:** fully contains "Tally," an active USPTO-registered fintech mark plus a well-known (if now-retired) debt-app brand — background confusion risk even with no exact-string collision.
