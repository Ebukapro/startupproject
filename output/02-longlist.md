# Agent 2 — Longlist: 120 Candidates

Generated 2026-09-17. Model: Opus (per routing table).

**Generation posture.** The USD 200 ceiling drives everything. Short real English
nouns and 4–5 letter coinages are not generated at all — their `.com` domains are
universally held by investors and unobtainable at this budget. Weight sits on
6–9 letter coinages and two-word compounds, the two reliable routes to a free `.com`.

**Hard constraints applied during generation.** Anything failing constraints 1–8
was not written down. Candidates that *pass* but carry a known risk worth
recording downstream are marked in the Flags column rather than silently dropped —
Agents 4–7 adjudicate those.

Flag key: `TM?` possible trademark/company collision · `PRON` pronunciation risk ·
`ECHO` echoes an incumbent · `LONG` length penalty for App Store title budget ·
`SEM` semantic/regulatory note.

**Honest assessment of Territory E.** This is the weakest territory in the set.
Respelling buys a domain at the direct cost of the spelling test in Agent 4 brief
4 — "heard once in a voice note, would a listener type it correctly into app store
search?" Ledjer, Tallee, Parrity and Klarity all fail that test by construction:
the listener will type the *correct* spelling and find nothing. For a bootstrapped,
word-of-mouth brand this is close to disqualifying. I expect few or none of these
to reach the final ten, and I recommend against forcing one in for territory spread.
