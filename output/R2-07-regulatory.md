## Judgment calls (as requested)

**(c) Anchorbasis** — MEDIUM. The stability metaphor is exactly the pattern SEC Nigeria and Apple reviewers now scrutinize post-Terra/Anchor Protocol. Name itself isn't a violation, but it invites a capital-protection reading that must be neutralized in marketing copy, not just the name.

**(d) Holdlot / Steerlot** — Split. "Hold" is a standard, neutral trading verb (buy/sell/hold) with no management connotation → LOW. "Steer" implies the platform is directing outcomes on the user's behalf, closer to discretionary management → MEDIUM. Recommend keeping Holdlot, dropping or caveat-ing Steerlot.

**(f) Light metaphors (Dawnlot, Candlelot, Torchlot, Lanternlot, Beaconlot)** — LOW, all five. These are fanciful/illumination branding (common across fintech generally, e.g. lighthouse/lantern-style names used by non-advisory apps) rather than a functional claim of advice. They don't use verbs like "advise," "recommend," or "signal." Same logic applies to Compasslot/Compassbasis (directional, not advisory). Residual note: if marketing later pairs any of these with words like "guidance," "signals," or "recommendations," that combination — not the name — would cross into investment-advice territory; a naming-only screen doesn't need to block them now.
