## Counts
PASS: 2 · CAUTION: 6 · FAIL: 8

## Every FAIL, one line each
- **Dorvinne** — reads as a pharmaceutical/drug name, not a person; high spelling risk.
- **Trebanna** — collides with Trebor (real UK sweets brand) and evokes "trebuchet"; siege-weapon/fantasy undertone.
- **Tervissa** — same homophone-collapse trap as the already-cut Melvissa (Teresa/Clarissa family); critical spelling fail.
- **Turvella** — carries the "topsy-turvy" comic undertone, wrong association for a money app.
- **Danvellis** — reads as "Dan + surname," like meeting a person, not naming a brand; Danvers/horror-asylum echo for some.
- **Ferrenis** — ends in "-enis," a near-homophone of a body part; guaranteed joke bait for a young, word-of-mouth audience.
- **Halvenis** — same anatomical-ending problem, compounded by "halve" (the worst possible word near people's savings).
- **Tarvenis** — same anatomical-ending problem, compounded by "tar" (sticky/negative connotation).
- **Ulvenna** — plays as a fantasy/video-game wolf-name, not a bank; uneven pronunciation across NG ethnic groups.

## Ranked top 10 (discriminating — quality craters after #8)
1. **Elowena** — cleanest across all six tests; sounds like a real, trustable person's name.
2. **Kestanna** — pronounceable, no meaning hits, best brand cadence after Elowena.
3. **Kerwenna** — genuine Celtic-name shape; only real weakness is the doubled n on spelling.
4. **Vestanna** — mythic-adjacent (Vesta), warm sound; "vest" as a visible English word is the only drag.
5. **Hestenna** — same mythic register as Vestanna, slightly less familiar but still workable.
6. **Cordenna** — solid sound; minor Corden-surname echo and "cord" visibility keep it at caution.
7. **Danvellis** — likeable "Dan" opening, but reads as a person's full name rather than a single brand word.
8. **Fenmara** — pleasant on the ear, but "Mara" = bitter is a real biblical association for a NG Christian audience.
9. *(below this line, not recommended — listed only to complete the ranking)* **Tervissa** — nicest-sounding of the FAILs, but the Melissa/Clarissa homophone trap is disqualifying.
10. **Trebanna** — workable sound, sunk mainly by the Trebor Mints collision.

**Bottom line: only Elowena and Kestanna are clean PASSes. The six CAUTIONs are usable in a pinch but each carries a real flaw. Recommend killing all three "-enis" names immediately regardless of any other analysis — the anatomical-ending problem is severe and applies identically to all three.**
